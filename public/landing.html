Chapter 4

How does it actually work?

01

Multiple AI agents

Not just one AI—a whole team. Each specializes in something different. One checks image quality. Another transcribes. One extracts medications. Another runs safety checks. They work together, catch each other's mistakes, and make sure nothing slips through.

02

Trained on real prescriptions

We fed it thousands of actual prescriptions. The good, the bad, and the "is this even English?" It learned that "BD" means twice daily, "TDS" means three times, "PRN" means as needed. All those cryptic abbreviations? It knows them by heart.

BD → Twice daily
TDS → Three times
PRN → As needed
QID → Four times
03

Safety checks built in

The AI doesn't just read—it thinks. If something looks off (like "take 47 pills at once" or a dosage that seems wrong), it flags it. Better to double-check than to guess wrong.
